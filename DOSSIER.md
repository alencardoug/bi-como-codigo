## 7. Where I hesitated

Decisions that could have gone the other way, with the reason they went this one.

- **The client (Q1).** psycopg 3 adds a runtime dependency. `psql` in the container would add
  none, but it fixes the container's name and turns every value into text. The plan recommends
  psycopg and leaves the choice to the Owner.
- **Totals over additive columns only (§7).** N78's `sums` could cover every numeric column as a
  pure checksum. I left out the non-additive ones so that no sum of an average or of P01's
  `order_count` exists anywhere, even as a checksum. The cost is that a corruption confined to a
  non-additive column is caught only by the round trip, not by `verify` in CI.
- **The round trip compares with the transaction's rows, not with a second read (§6).** A second
  read would test the database twice. The first read is what was written, so it is the right side
  of the equality for M19. The fingerprint against M59 is the outside check.
- **No `site/` in 2.4 (Q3).** The criterion asks for a package, not a publication. Writing it into
  `site/` would put real ciphertext in previews whose pages are still fixtures.
- **The grain from `view_rules` with `identity` (UQ-18).** M56 checked repeated keys at `eb11709`,
  not at the pin's data. The plan has the live run check them again, and refuses on any
  duplicate.
