## 7. Where I hesitated

Decisions that could have gone the other way, with the reason they went this one.

- **N102, judging only the current harness's records** (Claude Code's choice under the Owner's N101):
  the alternative was to keep the verdict over every record and accept that Evidence's old records,
  from before A8, left it "not established". I moved old records into `state["history"]` on a harness
  change. The risk to check: that a record of the current harness can be moved out, or an old one
  judged.
- **The controls inspect only what they add** (`builder_controls.py`): assembly proves the rest of
  the artifact byte for byte the builder output, which is inspected once. The alternative, a full
  inspection per control, made Evidence's run too long to finish. The risk: a control whose finding
  appears outside the added file is missed — `codes_elsewhere` is reported, and was empty.
- **The gate is not made faster**: `contract.inspect` searches each of 141,733 needles over each
  file, about 12 minutes per `verify` of Evidence's artifact. Making it a multi-pattern search would
  change `src/bi_como_codigo`, the product, for the prototype's sake; I recorded the time as
  Evidence's cost instead.
- **Quiescence only between priming journeys**: the measured journey is the last of its Chrome
  process, so no quiescence is waited after it; waiting after it cost Evidence 300 s per journey
  because its service worker stays a target.
- **The browser check reads filters by label**, and skips tables inside a `<dialog>`: both are
  adaptations to Evidence's markup (`%` for "all", the full-screen copy of a DataTable). The risk is
  that the reader is shaped to pass Evidence; the comparator's proofs refuse altered readings, but
  they alter the reading, not the markup.
- **Zero order sums are excluded from the order-sums check**, and the detail header is checked only
  when the table has rows or a header (`check.py`): a cell with no sales shows the notice instead.
